What are Hardcore Worlds?

Hardcore Worlds are a members-only game mode where each character gets one life. They run on their own servers, with no XP or drop rate boosts and a separate economy from the main game. They have their own Hiscores under Game Modes.

Unlike ironmen, Hardcore World characters are normal main accounts, so they can trade, use the Grand Exchange and do group PvM with each other. Their GE is separate from the main game's, which is why prices differ.

How to start on a Hardcore World

  • Start on Tutorial Island and speak to Death's Delegate near the Magic Instructor's house.
  • That unlocks Death's Domain, where you speak to Death to accept his challenge.
  • Every player starts with one free escape crystal. More cost 10,000 GP each from Death, Rick at the Wizards' Tower, or the Mysterious Stranger in Ver Sinhaza.

What happens when you die

A death is handled in two steps. First it works like a normal death: you keep 3 items, or 4 with Protect Item, and PvP deaths follow the usual Wilderness rules. Then you choose Reset or Transfer.

Reset

  • Wipes all progress: quests, skills, items, and the items you protected.
  • You can reset as many times as you like.
  • After each reset there's a 3-day cooldown on getting GP from the Stronghold of Security.

Transfer

  • Permanently removes the character from Hardcore Worlds.
  • You keep your protected items and everything in your bank and storage.

Either way, items that would have gone to your gravestone are deleted.

Activities that count as a real death

Hardcore Worlds use the same rules as Hardcore Group Ironman. These are unsafe: Barbarian Assault, the Camelot training room, Chambers of Xeric, the Dream World in Lunar Diplomacy, Fishing Trawler, the Inferno, Magic Training Arena, Nightmare Zone, Pest Control, dying in a player-owned house, the PvM Arena, the TzHaar Fight Cave (except with the elite diary resurrection), random events and the Rogues' Den maze.

Safe activities

Capsizing a boat (other boat deaths are unsafe), Castle Wars, Clan Wars, Emir's Arena, the fourth phase of the Fremennik Trials fight with Koschei, Last Man Standing (disabled at launch), Pete Kayer's Challenges, the Galvek and Glough boss replays, Soul Wars, the TzHaar Fight Pit, and Zulrah only with the Elite Western Provinces Diary resurrection.

Broadcasts and World Firsts

Big moments are broadcast to every Hardcore World player, such as deaths of players past certain milestones and World First achievements. World Firsts cover quests, bosses and skill milestones, and more are being revealed after launch.

Hardcore Worlds FAQ

Do Hardcore Worlds have their own GE prices?

Yes. The economy is separate from the main game, so the same item can be much dearer or cheaper. The OSRS Wiki tracks Hardcore World trades separately, and the table above shows them live.

Can you trade on Hardcore Worlds?

Yes. Hardcore World characters are main accounts, not ironmen, so they can trade other Hardcore World players and use the Grand Exchange.

Can you flip on Hardcore Worlds?

The same GE tax applies, so a flip needs a margin bigger than the 2% tax. Sort the table by "Biggest margin" to find them, and remember fewer players means lower volume than the main game. Our merchanting guide covers the basics.
