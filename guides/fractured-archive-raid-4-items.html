Raid 4 items: Fractured Archive rewards and prices

Every new item from The Fractured Archive, with live Grand Exchange prices, what each one does, and whether Ascension bolts are worth making.

Updated 21 October 2026

The Fractured Archive is Old School RuneScape's fourth raid. It opened on 20 October 2026, and its rewards hit the Grand Exchange the same day. Five of them are tradeable uniques, and the rarest sell for billions of GP while supply is still tiny.

The new uniques

Obligator

A two-handed crush weapon that needs 85 Attack, and the raid's megarare. It rolls its damage several times based on how weak the target is to crush compared with stab and slash, and keeps the best roll, so it hits hardest on monsters with low crush defence. It is charged with Ascension shards and sells uncharged. It was called "The Breaker" in the reward poll before being renamed. Obligator (uncharged) price chart.

Zorya's tome

A two-handed powered tome for Magic, needing 85 Magic. It attacks every three ticks and has a special attack with much higher accuracy and damage, which makes it the first Magic weapon with a proper damage special. It was polled as the "Gatekeeper's cane". Zorya's tome (uncharged) price chart.

Ascension crossbows

A pair of dual-wielded crossbows that needs 80 Ranged. They fire faster than any other crossbow and use the raid's own Ascension bolts, so expect bolt demand to grow as more pairs enter the game. Ascension crossbows price chart.

Rondache

A melee shield that needs 80 Defence. It gives Strength like an Avernic defender but no accuracy bonuses, and has its own shield bash effect. Rondache price chart.

Horn of Bran (Zeal prayer)

The Horn of Bran unlocks Zeal, a stronger version of Piety that needs 84 Prayer. Zeal raises Attack by 25%, Strength by 28% and Defence by 25%, against Piety's 20%, 23% and 25%. Like the Royal Titans' prayer scrolls, the horn is tradeable until it is used, so every account that wants Zeal has to buy or get one. Horn of Bran price chart.

Ascension shards and bolts

Ascension shards come from raid chests and from mining the Ascension rocks near the entrance. With 86 Fletching you can cut one shard into 10 Ascension bolts with a regular chisel (a jeweller's chisel doesn't work), then add 10 onyx or diamond bolt tips to make 10 tipped bolts. Shards also charge the Obligator and the Archivist's folio.

Untradeable rewards

  • Elemental fragments each add 2 to your base max hit with spells of their element. You won't get the same element twice.
  • The Archivist's folio teleports you back to the raid and is charged with Ascension shards.
  • The first three teams to finish the raid earned gold, silver and bronze medals.

Bosses

Each run is five fights. The first and last are always the same, and for the middle three the team picks one of two bosses:

  • First: the Archive Guardian
  • Second: Ezekiel or Bork and the Smorks
  • Third: Moia or Dreadnoth the Jellied
  • Fourth: Dagon'hai or Xarth
  • Final: Ocellus

Getting there

You need to have finished While Guthix Sleeps. After the quest a messenger finds you with a note pointing to the entrance, a cliff face near the Ancient Guthixian Temple under Lumbridge. The raid scales from 2 to 8 players and Jagex suggests 3 to 5. You can go in alone, but it scales as if you were a team of two. There was no practice mode at launch.

Buying or flipping raid 4 items

New raid uniques nearly always open at their highest price and fall as more teams finish the raid and supply builds up. If you only want to use an item, waiting usually costs you less. If you flip, the margins on billion-GP items are large but only a handful trade each day, so an offer can sit for hours while the price moves under it. Ascension bolts and shards trade in the tens of thousands a day and are much easier to flip. Questions

What are the Raid 4 items?

The five tradeable uniques are the Obligator, Zorya's tome, the Ascension crossbows, the Rondache and the Horn of Bran. The raid also adds Ascension shards, Ascension bolts in plain, onyx and diamond versions, Elemental fragments and the Archivist's folio.

Which Fractured Archive rewards are tradeable?

Everything in the price table above: the five uniques, the shards and every kind of Ascension bolt. Elemental fragments and the Archivist's folio are untradeable.

How do you unlock the Zeal prayer?

Use a Horn of Bran from the raid. Zeal needs 84 Prayer and replaces Piety on your prayer book.

What do you need to enter The Fractured Archive?

Only the quest While Guthix Sleeps is confirmed. Some guides list a Sailing level too, but Jagex hasn't announced one.
