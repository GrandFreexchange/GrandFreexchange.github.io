How to import tile markers

  1. In RuneLite, open the plugin list and make sure Ground Markers is on.
  2. Copy a tile pack with its Copy button.
  3. In game, right-click the world map orb next to the minimap and choose Import Ground Markers.
  4. Say Yes when it asks to import the markers. They appear as soon as you are in that area.

No Import option on the orb? In the Ground Markers settings, turn on Show import/export/clear options. Importing adds to the markers you already have. Clear on the same menu removes the markers in the area you are standing in.

Preview your own tile markers

Paste markers you exported from RuneLite, or got from a friend, to see the layout before you import them. Nothing you paste leaves your browser.

Tile marker questions

What are tile markers in OSRS?

Tile markers are coloured squares you place on the ground with RuneLite's Ground Markers plugin. Players use them to mark safe spots, walking routes and where to stand in boss fights. Only you can see them.

Are tile markers allowed?

Yes. Ground Markers is one of RuneLite's built-in plugins, and RuneLite is an approved third-party client. The markers only change what you see.

How do I share my own tile markers?

Right-click the world map orb and choose Export Ground Markers. RuneLite copies the markers for the area you are in as JSON, which anyone can import the same way. You can check the layout with the preview on this page before you share it.

Why can't I see the markers after importing?

Markers belong to the area they were made in, so they only show once you are there. In instanced fights such as raids they show once the instance loads. Check that Ground Markers is turned on and its fill opacity isn't set to 0.
