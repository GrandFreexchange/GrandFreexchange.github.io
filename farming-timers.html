    How farming timers work in OSRS

    Crops in Old School RuneScape don't grow on a stopwatch from the moment you plant them. They grow in stages, and each stage only moves on at a growth tick. Herbs tick every 20 minutes, trees every 40, fruit trees every 160 and hardwoods every 640. Every player's ticks are shifted by a small personal offset, so two friends planting at the same time can see their crops finish a few minutes apart.

    That's why each timer shows a ready by time: by then the crop is certainly grown. It is often ready a little sooner, by up to one growth tick (20 minutes for herbs). Bird houses are different: they fill 50 minutes after you set them, so their timer is exact.

    Your crops keep growing while you are logged out. Timers here keep counting when you close the tab, and alerts pop up while the page is open in any tab.
