How the Herblore calculator works

Level 1 to 99 Herblore takes 13,034,431 XP, which is 148,965 prayer potions. Enter your current level or XP and your goal, and the table shows how many of each item you need and what it costs right now on the Grand Exchange. Prices are the latest real trades from the OSRS Wiki price API, the same data as the osrs.lol price dashboard.

Each potion is a clean herb, a vial of water and the secondary ingredient, making one 3-dose potion. Ingredients are priced at the instant-buy price and potions at the instant-sell price.

XP is for making the finished potion from the herb, including the unfinished potion step.

Making money from Herblore

Sort by Profit to see which potions are worth making today. Prayer potions, super restores and Saradomin brews sell fast because players use them for bossing.

Cheapest XP

Sort by GP/XP. A negative number is what each XP costs you, a positive one means you make money while you train.

Buy limits

Herbs and secondaries have GE buy limits, so for big batches you may need to buy over several 4-hour windows. Check an ingredient on its osrs.lol price page for its limit.
