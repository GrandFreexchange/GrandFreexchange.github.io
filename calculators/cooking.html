How the Cooking calculator works

Level 1 to 99 Cooking takes 13,034,431 XP, which is 68,603 karambwans. Enter your current level or XP and your goal, and the table shows how many of each item you need and what it costs right now on the Grand Exchange. Prices are the latest real trades from the OSRS Wiki price API, the same data as the osrs.lol price dashboard.

The numbers assume nothing burns. At low levels you will burn some food, so you need a little more than shown.

Jug of wine is grapes plus a jug of water. It gives its XP when the wine finishes fermenting.

Fastest Cooking XP

1-tick karambwans from level 30 and wines from level 35 are the fastest methods. Wines are made from your bank, so you can train them anywhere.

Cooking for profit

Sort by Profit. Raw fish that sell for less than the cooked fish make money, but the margin moves with the market, so check before you buy in bulk.

Burning food

Every fish has a level where you stop burning it, lower with Cooking gauntlets or at the Hosidius kitchen. Until then budget some extra fish.
