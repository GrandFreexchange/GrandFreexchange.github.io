How the Prayer calculator works

Level 1 to 99 Prayer takes 13,034,431 XP, which is 51,724 dragon bones on a Gilded altar. Enter your current level or XP and your goal, and the table shows how many of each item you need and what it costs right now on the Grand Exchange. Prices are the latest real trades from the OSRS Wiki price API, the same data as the osrs.lol price dashboard.

Prices are for the bones or ashes only. The Ectofuntus also needs a pot and a bucket of slime per bone, and the offering spells use runes.

On the Chaos altar in the Wilderness each bone has a 50% chance not to be used, so on average a bone gives twice the XP. Bring only what you can afford to lose.

Which altar should I use?

A Gilded altar in a player-owned house with both burners lit gives 350% XP and is safe, so it is the usual choice. The Chaos altar gives the same 350% and keeps half your bones on average, which halves the cost, but it is in level 38 Wilderness. The Ectofuntus gives the most XP per bone (400%) but is slow.

Cheapest bones per XP
