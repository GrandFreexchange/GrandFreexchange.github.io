How the Construction calculator works

Level 1 to 99 Construction takes 13,034,431 XP, which is 15,518 mahogany tables. Enter your current level or XP and your goal, and the table shows how many of each item you need and what it costs right now on the Grand Exchange. Prices are the latest real trades from the OSRS Wiki price API, the same data as the osrs.lol price dashboard.

Costs count planks (and nails for the bookcase) only, not a butler or the Plank Make spell. Removing furniture you built gives the materials back for the mythical cape, so the cape is not counted.

The sawmill option uses the log price plus the sawmill fee: 100 gp for a plank, 250 for oak, 500 for teak and 1,500 for mahogany.

Fastest way to level

Oak larders from level 33 and mahogany tables from 52 are the usual training methods, with a demon butler fetching planks. Gnome benches give the same XP as mahogany tables and are quicker to build in a garden.

Buying planks or logs

Switch the method above to compare buying planks with buying logs and paying the sawmill fee. Whichever is cheaper changes with the market.

Mythical cape

Building and removing a mythical cape mount uses 3 teak planks for 370 XP. It needs the Dragon Slayer II cape, which you get back.
