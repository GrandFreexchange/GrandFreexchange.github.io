Buy broken is what the broken pieces cost, Sell repaired is what the repaired item sells for after the 2% GE tax. Instant buys at the latest instant-buy price and sell at the instant-sell price; patient offers buy and sell at the other end of the margin, which is cheaper but slower. Traded / day is how many of the broken item changed hands on the GE in the last 24 hours. Click a column to sort.

How armour repair flipping works

Barrows equipment can only be traded when it is fully repaired or fully broken (the "0" versions). Broken pieces often sell for less than a repaired piece minus the repair fee, so you can buy them on the Grand Exchange, repair them, and sell them back. This calculator does that sum for every piece using the latest real trades from the OSRS Wiki price API, the same data as the osrs.lol price dashboard.

Bob in Lumbridge, Dunstan in Burthorpe and the other repair NPCs charge the full price. Repairing at an armour stand in your player-owned house (level 55 Construction in the workshop) takes 0.5% off for every Smithing level, so at level 99 it is just over half price. Boosted Smithing levels count.

Blood moon, Blue moon and Eclipse moon armour

The Moons of Peril armour from the Lunar Chest degrades like Barrows equipment and breaks after about 45 hours of combat. Repairing a broken helm, chestplate or tassets costs 1,500,000 with an NPC, or 757,500 at an armour stand with 99 Smithing. Broken moon pieces trade in small numbers, so check Traded / day before you buy.

Torva armour

Damaged Torva from Nex is repaired with Bandosian components at level 90 Smithing: one for the full helm and two each for the platebody and platelegs. The calculator prices the components at the live GE price, and greys out Torva below level 90.

Buying pieces or whole sets

The armour set rows buy all four broken pieces of one brother (or the three Torva pieces), repair them, and sell them as a set from the Grand Exchange clerk, which packs them for free. Sets sometimes sell for more than the pieces added up, sometimes for less, so compare the set row with its pieces.

Tips

Margins move quickly and many broken pieces trade only a few times a day, so a patient offer can take hours to fill. Buy limits apply to broken pieces too. The GE tax is 2% of the sell price, capped at 5m per item, and is already taken off.
