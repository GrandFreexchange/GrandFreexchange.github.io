Profit Calculation

Profit is calculated using the following formula:

(Sell Price - Buy Price) * Qty - GE Tax

The Grand Exchange (GE) tax is 2% of each item's sale price, rounded down and capped at 5m GP per item. Bonds and a few low-level tools (like chisels and hammers) are tax-free.
